I am a Researcher at **Fujitsu Research**, working on reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, LLM agent fine-tuning, and AI security. I completed my Master's in Artificial Intelligence at **IIT Hyderabad**, and previously worked as a Researcher in cyber security at **TCS Research**.

My recent work includes research on reward design for reinforcement learning in sparse-reward settings, presented at ICLR 2026, alongside earlier work on federated learning and data privacy.
