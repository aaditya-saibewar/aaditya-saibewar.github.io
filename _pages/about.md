I am a Researcher in Physical AI Lab at Fujitsu Research, working on robotics and theoretical problems in memory, long-horizon task enhancement, and failure recovery in robotics. My current research also explores constrained optimization using Wasserstein shortest paths for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and reinforcement learning tasks.

Prior to this, I was part of the AI Security Lab, where my focus was red-teaming GenAI systems, designing novel jailbreak attacks and building defenses against state-of-the-art jailbreaks, particularly social engineering-based attacks.

Before that, I worked at TCS Research in cybersecurity LAB, starting out in federated learning setups and later moving into explainability and robustness for vision applications.

I completed my Master's in Artificial Intelligence at IIT Hyderabad, where my thesis focused on optimization — specifically, conditional optimal transport.

Beyond my core work, I'm interested in graph machine learning, reinforcement learning and optimization for generative models. More broadly, my interests span multiple areas of machine learning, including both empirical and theoretical research on LLMs, as well as ML applications in healthcare and finance.

Outside of research, I enjoy traveling and sports especially football, cricket, table tennis, Running, and volleyball. Travel stories coming soon!
